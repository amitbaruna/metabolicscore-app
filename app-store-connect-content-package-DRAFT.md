## App Review notes

*(Paste directly into the "Notes" field for this version in App Store Connect)*

This app is a self-assessment and health-tracking tool. Two things worth clarifying up front:

**1. Coaching enrollment and payment (Guideline 3.1.3(e) — Goods and Services Outside of the App)**
The app displays informational pricing for the coach's 1:1 metabolic health coaching service. Enrollment and payment happen entirely outside the app — the user is redirected to WhatsApp to complete payment directly with the coach. No purchase, checkout, or payment flow of any kind occurs inside the app. Once payment is confirmed externally, the user's account status updates from trial to paid, which enables a call-scheduling feature within the app. This feature only lets the user pick a time slot to coordinate a coaching call; the call itself takes place outside the app, by phone or Google Meet. No app content, features, or digital goods are sold, unlocked, or delivered via in-app purchase — the only paid deliverable is the coaching service itself, consumed entirely outside the app.

**2. Apple Health ("Connect Apple Health")**
The app includes an entry point for Apple Health integration that is intentionally inactive in this release, displaying "Apple Health sync is coming in a future update." No HealthKit permission is requested, no HealthKit data is read or written, and no HealthKit entitlement is present in this build — this feature is fully planned for a future release, not partially implemented.
