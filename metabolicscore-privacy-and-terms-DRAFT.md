## PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated:** [FILL: date of publish]

### 1. Who we are

Metabolic Score is operated by Amit Baruna ("we," "us," "our"), a metabolic health coach based in India. This policy explains what information the Metabolic Score app and website (metabolicscore.in) collect, how it's used, and the choices you have.

### 2. Information we collect

**Account information:** name, email address, and basic profile details you provide when creating an account.

**Health-related information you choose to share:** responses to our metabolic health assessment, self-reported symptoms and their severity, cravings you log, medical conditions you disclose, body composition details you provide (e.g. age, body fat distribution), and your assessment scores and history over time.

**What we do *not* currently collect:** as of this version of the app, we do not access Apple Health, Google Fit, or any wearable device data. If this changes in a future update, we will update this policy and request your explicit consent before any such data is accessed.

**Usage information:** how you interact with the app (e.g. features used, general engagement), collected to improve the product and diagnose technical issues.

**Technical/crash data:** basic device and error information collected automatically to help us fix bugs, via a third-party crash-reporting tool (Sentry). This is scrubbed of personal health details before being sent.

### 3. How we use your information

- To generate your personalized metabolic health assessment, score, and insights.
- To track your progress over time and show you meaningful changes.
- To communicate with you about your account, your assessment results, and (if you opt in) coaching-related updates.
- To improve the app and fix problems.

We do **not** sell your personal or health information to third parties.

### 4. Coaching enrollment and payment

If you choose to enroll in 1:1 coaching, enrollment and payment are handled entirely **outside this app**, directly with Amit Baruna via WhatsApp. No payment information is collected or processed within the app itself. Once payment is confirmed externally, your account status is updated to reflect your enrollment, which unlocks a call-scheduling feature inside the app. Coaching calls themselves take place outside the app (by phone or video call).
